# Design System

## Overview

KOHYAMAデジタル総研 is a dark, premium portfolio site for AI utilization, web production, and business-growth support. The identity uses a deep navy base, luminous blue hero imagery, and restrained gold accents to signal speed, trust, and practical outcomes. Layouts are section-based and editorial: full-viewport hero, two-column service narrative, proof badges, portfolio cards, policy panels, and a four-step workflow. The tone is Japanese business-support professional rather than SaaS dashboard.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#0A0E1A` — main deep navy background.
- **Secondary Surface**: `#0F1628` — card and panel background.
- **Deep Surface**: `#080B14` — near-black depth behind hero and overlays.
- **Content White**: `#F4F6FB` — main readable text.
- **Pure White**: `#FFFFFF` — glow highlights and logo light.
- **Gold Accent**: `#E8C547` — section headings, dividers, bullets, CTA emphasis.
- **Gold Deep**: `#C9A84C` — borders and subdued brand lines.
- **Blue Accent**: `#1A365D` — cold AI glow and hero support tones.
- **Soft Surface**: `#EEF1F6` — light surfaces inside captured portfolio assets.

## Typography

- **Montserrat** (400, 500, 600, 700, 800): English labels, section titles, CTA-style text, and compact UI labels.
- **Noto Sans JP** (400, 500, 600, 700): body copy, service lists, workflow labels, and readable Japanese captions.
- **Noto Serif JP** (500, 700, 900): emotional hero statements and high-impact Japanese phrases.
- Hero typography is bold and cinematic, while cards use smaller, dense business text. Video text should keep Japanese lines short and avoid cramped paragraphs.

## Elevation

Depth comes from dark layered surfaces, thin gold borders, luminous blue glows, and image cards rather than heavy drop shadows. Cards sit on `#0F1628` with subtle border treatment and rounded corners; hero assets use bloom, light trails, and a mountain-like AI beam as the signature depth device.

## Components

- **Cinematic AI Hero**: Full-viewport image with glowing blue mountain, gold UI lines, and a large serif-style Japanese message.
- **NexCore Proof Banner**: Horizontal client banner mixing portrait, dark overlay, and gold headline text.
- **Reason Badges**: Compact proof labels such as fastest next-day delivery, 24-hour reply, funnel design, and repeat work.
- **Portfolio Cards**: Three-column dark cards showing thumbnails for YouTube/key visual, logo/branding, and flyer work.
- **Service Policy Panels**: Split panels with icon headings, service lists, tool explanations, and honest operating promises.
- **Workflow Steps**: Numbered four-step process from hearing to proposal, production, delivery, and operation support.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use `#0A0E1A` and `#0F1628` as the main stage, with `#E8C547` for controlled emphasis.
- Feature captured work assets generously; this brand proves itself visually.
- Keep motion luminous and directional: beams, scan lines, card cascades, and clean process reveals.
- Use Japanese copy that feels practical, direct, and client-oriented.
- Maintain a high-contrast hierarchy between hero phrases, proof points, and supporting cards.

### Don'ts

- Do not switch to a bright generic SaaS palette.
- Do not replace the site's captured images with abstract stock graphics.
- Do not overuse large paragraphs in-frame; keep Japanese text short and readable.
- Do not make motion cute or playful; the site is premium, focused, and businesslike.
- Do not use generic purple-blue gradients as the main identity; the accent language is navy, gold, and electric blue.
